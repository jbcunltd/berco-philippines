Berco site measure standard

Draft for Jumbo, 25 Nov 2026. Built from the German kitchen industry checklist (AMK), IKEA's measurer list and trade check-measure practice, adapted for overseas production.

Why this matters for us: our cabinets are made overseas. A mistake found at installation cannot be re-cut locally. It means a re-order and weeks of waiting. Every rule below exists to catch that mistake on site.

Three rules above all: all numbers in mm · measure from ONE reference corner per wall · never leave with only photos.

0 · Before you go

  • MUSTAsk the client for the floor plan, the stage of works, and whether the floor tile is laid.
  • MUSTCondo? Ask for admin rules: work hours, lift booking, permits.

1 · Tools

  • MUSTLaser measure, plus a 5 m tape
  • MUST1.2 m spirit level or a laser level
  • MUSTCharged phone, plus the Berco measure sheet (or pad and pencil)
  • MUSTPainter's tape and a marker to label walls A, B, C
  • NICEStud and pipe finder, angle finder, power bank

2 · Sketch first

  • MUSTDraw the room. Label each wall A, B, C clockwise.
  • MUSTMark each wall concrete/hollow block or gypsum/board. Board needs backing for wall cabinets.

3 · Walls

  • MUSTMeasure each wall at 3 heights: 100 from the floor, 900 (worktop) and 2100. Write all 3.
  • MUSTDesign to the SMALLEST reading. The difference goes into fillers and scribe panels.
  • MUSTFloor to ceiling at both ends and the middle of each wall.
  • MUSTPlumb: level at each end of each wall. Write the gap, top versus bottom.
  • MUSTSquare: both room diagonals, or 3-4-5 in each corner at floor, 900 and ceiling.
  • MUSTFloor level along each run. Find the HIGH point and write how far the floor drops from it.
  • MUSTFlag on the sketch: plumb over 5, floor drop over 5, length spread over 5, any corner not square. (Thresholds are proposed. Confirm with our installer and the factory.)

4 · Locate and dimension

Every item gets 2 numbers: height from the floor, and distance from the reference corner. Add its size where it applies.

  • MUSTWindows: frame, sill height, sill depth, which way it opens and how far
  • MUSTDoors: frame width, swing direction, handle, the arc it clears
  • MUSTOutlets, switches, breaker panel
  • MUSTWater in (hot and cold), drain out and its diameter
  • MUSTGas point, or where the LPG tank will sit
  • MUSTRange hood duct exit and its diameter, or "no exit, recirculating"
  • MUSTAircon unit, its pipes and condensate drain, any water heater
  • MUSTBeams, columns, soffits, ceiling drops, skirting, cornices, window grilles
  • NICELight fixtures and ceiling access panels

5 · Appliances

  • MUSTModel number and W x H x D: fridge, hob, oven, hood, dishwasher, microwave, washer and dryer
  • MUSTFridge door swing and the side clearance it needs
  • NICEClient sends the brochure links the same day

6 · Photos

  • MUSTOne wide shot from each corner
  • MUSTEach wall straight on, with the tape or laser reading visible
  • MUSTClose-up of every service, with the tape in shot
  • MUSTThe level on the floor and on the wall wherever you flagged a problem
  • MUSTThe route from gate or lobby to the room: lift door, lift car, stairs, corridor turns
  • NICEA short video walk-through

7 · Site condition

  • MUSTStage: bare, plastered, tiled or finished. Photo of the floor.
  • MUSTFloor tile not laid yet? Get the finished floor level and tile thickness from the contractor, in writing.
  • MUSTDelivery: lift internal size, tightest door, stairs, parking, loading hours
  • MUSTAre services final? Anything the client plans to move goes in the notes.
  • NICESigns of damp or termites

8 · Ask the client

  • MUSTWho cooks, how often, what stays on the counter
  • MUSTWorktop height wanted. Height of the tallest user.
  • MUSTBudget range and target move-in date
  • MUSTContractor or architect, and their number
  • NICEStorage pains today: pots, bins, rice, appliances

9 · Before you leave, tell the client

  • MUSTThe date the design and quote will arrive (within 5 working days)
  • MUSTThat a check measure happens before the factory order
  • MUSTThat pipes, outlets and walls must not move after sign-off, or the order is at risk
  • MUSTThat lead time starts after sign-off and deposit (figure per finish to be set, fix list D4)

10 · Check measure, before the factory order

  • MUSTOnly once walls are plastered, services are final and the floor finish or its level is fixed
  • MUSTRe-measure every wall at 3 heights, every service, plumb and level. Compare to the drawing.
  • MUSTAny difference over 3 means the drawing is revised and re-approved (proposed)
  • MUSTA second person checks the drawing against the site sheet
  • MUSTThe client signs the final drawing. No signature, no factory order.

Where this came from

  • Backbone: AMK Aufmaß-Checkliste Küche, the German kitchen industry body behind Nobilia and Häcker (2018). PDF
  • Services list: IKEA measurer checklist. IKEA GB · IKEA Canada
  • Plumb, square, level: JLC · Fine Homebuilding · Woodweb
  • Check measure: Decor Cabinets Academy · Australian training unit MSFKB3003 · Eagle Woodworking
  • Berco layer (ours): smallest reading wins, hollow block vs board walls, aircon and LPG, untiled floors, condo rules, the signed-drawing gate.
  • Not found publicly: Oppolia, Oppein, Häcker, Nobilia, California Closets or NKBA's own measuring forms.
