What we're actually chasing

Soul Cinema, generated from scratch

this morning's test
THE LOOK

Real grain structure, light with a direction, shadows that hold detail, depth of field that falls off naturally.

The finding

  • You cannot grade catalogue lighting into cinema lighting. Our supplier renders are lit flat and even, with everything in focus, because that is how product catalogues are lit. There is no directional light in the file to bring out — grading can only push what is already there.
  • The "looks real" quality comes from composing the frame as a photograph in the first place — a camera position, a light source with a direction, a chosen focal plane. Soul does that at generation time. No post process adds it afterwards.
  • So the choice is a real one, not a technical gap: keep the renders and accept they look like renders, or generate fresh in Soul Cinema and accept the geometry is illustrative rather than sourced.
  • The grade is still worth applying — it costs nothing and it takes a little of the digital edge off. Just don't expect it to do the heavy lifting.
