Production spec, whichever you pick
90 × 54 mm trim · 3 mm bleed · 5 mm safe margin
Type: Libre Bodoni (name) + Jost (everything else) — the print-licensed equivalents of Bodoni 72 and Futura. Bodoni 72 and Futura are macOS system fonts and are not licensed for commercial print, so they stay off the artwork.
Colour: ground #FDFBF7 · ink #2B2620 · beige #F0E7D8 · champagne #C9A863, dropping to #9A7A38 wherever it becomes small text so it stays readable.
Marks: the real Berco lockup and the real JBC lockup, recoloured from their master files — letterforms untouched. Both resolve past 1,700 dpi at card size.
Stock suggestion: 350 gsm uncoated, soft matte. A gloss laminate would fight the cream.
