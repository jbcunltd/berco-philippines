Retractable Awnings

Motorised shade that extends when you need cover and retracts to give the sky back. Sized to the opening, mounted to the structure that can carry it.

Four case types

The case is the decision.

All four use the same acrylic-coated fabric — water- and UV-resistant. What changes is how much the housing protects the mechanism when the awning is closed.

Full Case

The cassette closes fully around the fabric and arms when retracted — the most protection from sun, dust and rain when the awning is not in use.

Luxury Case

Full-case protection with the heavier-duty build and a wider maximum span. Our specification for exposed frontages and commercial sites.

Half Case

A partial hood covering the roller. Lighter and shallower than a full case — a middle option where the awning is used often.

Non-Case

Open roller, no cassette. The simplest and most compact build, suited to sheltered positions and covered walkways.

Before you choose

What decides whether this works

  • The mounting surface has to carry the load — we check the wall or structure before quoting.
  • Retractable awnings are for sun and light rain. They are retracted in strong wind, and we will tell you the limit for your unit and site.
  • Maximum width and projection depend on the case type and the opening.
  • Exposure matters — a frontage taking full afternoon sun and driven rain needs a different build from a sheltered balcony.
