Louver Pergola

Adjustable aluminium louvers on a fixed frame. Angle them through the day to steer the light; close them when the weather turns.

Four builds

Built to the space.

Every build uses the same principle — rotating aluminium blades with an integrated gutter in the frame. What changes is the structure, the span and how much customisation the site needs.

Standard

The core louver pergola. Motorised blades, integrated roof gutter, sized for courtyards, pool decks and garden seating.

Luxury

Heavier extrusion, waterproof motor and integrated LED lighting in the beams. Our specification where the pergola is the centrepiece of the space.

Heavy Duty — Lifestyle

A customised build for larger residential spans and irregular layouts, with vertical shading and glass as options.

Heavy Duty — Engineering

Wall-mounted and engineered configurations for commercial sites, alfresco areas and structures that have to tie into an existing building.

Before you choose

What decides whether this works

  • Louver pergolas are a fixed structure — footings, posts and the surface they land on all have to be right, so we check the site first.
  • Closed louvers shed rain into the frame's internal gutter. Drainage has to have somewhere to go; we plan that with you.
  • Blade span, post spacing and the total covered area are all linked — a wider span is not simply a bigger order.
  • We will give you the wind and load figures for the exact configuration quoted for your site, not a generic number.
