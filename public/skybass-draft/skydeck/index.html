SkyDeck

Retractable Pergola

A roof that opens. A motorised PVC canopy runs on an aluminium frame — closed for shade and rain, retracted for open sky.

HERO NEEDED — SkyDeck canopy mid-travel over a terrace or pool
How it is built

Built to the space.

One system, configured to the space. The canopy travels along the frame, so the same structure gives you full cover or full sky.

Retractable PVC roof

A waterproof membrane that opens and closes on the frame — the roof itself is the moving part.

Flat or arc

The frame is built flat or arched depending on the span, the drainage run and how the structure meets the building.

Wall-mounted, wall-hanging or free-standing

Three mounting approaches — the choice depends on what the existing structure can carry.

Optional LED lighting

Lighting integrated into the beams, so the space works after dark without adding fixtures.

Before you choose

What decides whether this works

  • The frame has to be founded or fixed to something that can carry it — roof decks in particular need checking before anything is quoted.
  • A retracting roof needs clear travel along its whole run; overhead obstructions change the design.
  • Drainage is designed in, not added later — where the water goes is part of the layout.
  • Numeric wind and waterproofing figures for this system are confirmed per project; we will not quote a general number that may not hold on your site.
