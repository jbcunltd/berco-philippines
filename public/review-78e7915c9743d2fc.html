Six scenes, built from one of our own designs

Berco — interior systems reel · 20 Aug 2026

I took one design off our website — the warm walnut kitchen — and reshot it on the cinema lens set instead of inventing a kitchen from scratch.

So the walnut, the lit bronze display cabinets, the handleless greige island and the marble are all ours. Only the light and the camera changed.

Tell me which scenes to keep and I'll put them in order for you to approve before anything moves.

The design I started from

From our kitchens page — “warm walnut kitchen cabinetry”. I trimmed off the fridge, the oven and the magazines before using it, so none of that could carry through.

What I caught and fixed

Three of the first six had problems. I checked every frame at full size, threw those three out and shot them again — these are the rejects, not the scenes above.

All six scenes above were re-checked at full resolution: no lettering, no engraving, no maker's marks, nothing branded in shot. Illustrative of our own design — not a photograph of a finished project.
