On getting real silver
What you see here is a printed grey. True silver is metallic stock — Stardream Silver or Curious Metallics, ink printed onto the paper — or silver foil. Metallic stock catches the light and is the version that actually looks like silver; a flat grey flood printed on ordinary uncoated stock tends to look mottled and shows every scuff. If we go silver, I would specify the stock rather than print the colour.

What changed in the marks
All four wordmarks set in one ink (#1A1A1A), matching the card's own type — Berco's warm brown, Nautilus's blue-black and Artifax's green have been unified. Only Skybass's chevron keeps its colour.
Full lockups, used as drawn. Each brand's complete mark — name and slogan — which also retires the Skybass kerning problem entirely: that gap was only wrong because I had stripped the tagline and shrunk the chevron. The authentic lockup spacing is back.
Slogans in white, names in ink. At strip size the slogans measure 0.43–1.01 mm, roughly 1–3 pt. Set in ink they would fill in and read as a grey smudge; in white they read as a soft second layer nobody strains to decipher. ⚠ They will not be readable at this size — they are texture, not copy. A slogan meant to be read belongs on the back at four times this size.
Layout rebalanced. The JBC lockup moved to the top-right rather than being duplicated there — the same mark twice on one face reads as an error. That freed 10 mm of dead space above the brand strip, now carrying the offices line: real information a specifier wants, rather than padding.
Aligned on name baselines, not bounding boxes. The four lockups carry different slogan depths, so matching their outer boxes would have thrown the names out of line. Names are set to equal cap height (Berco +6% for its Didone) with their baselines on one line; the slogans hang below at each brand's own proportion.
Taglines removed from Berco and Nautilus for the strip: at this size they land near 2 pt and fill in on uncoated stock. Skybass's tagline was removed the same way; the chevron was kept.
Optical sizing: the strip is set to equal cap height, not equal width, with Berco 6% larger because a high-contrast Didone reads lighter than bold sans at the same size.

Spec: 90 × 54 mm trim · 3 mm bleed · 5 mm safe margin · Jost throughout · ink #1A1A1A. 350 gsm, soft matte.
