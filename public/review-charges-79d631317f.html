The text message does not say Bluehost. It says WEB***********. Compare it with the 25 Nov line, which spells out VERCEL INC. The merchant on these three is masked. Read the full merchant name off the CBC app's transaction list before you put Bluehost's name on a dispute form, because if it is a different merchant the claim goes somewhere else.

Two of the three may be holds, not real charges. An authorisation and a settled charge send an identical SMS. Holds fall off in three to seven days and never reach the statement. The app's transaction list is the truth here, not the texts.
