Four we refuse, and why

×

Animated stat counters

Their own live demo renders "14 Completed Projects, 3 Trained Professionals, 0 International Offices". A counter that shows zero is worse than no counter, and our real numbers are not all for publishing.

×

Flip cards

Broken on their own flagship demo at 375px. Both faces render stacked, so the front text sits on top of mirrored back text and neither reads. A hover effect has no hover on a phone.

×

Scroll-reveal animation

Scrolled at phone speed and hit entirely blank white screens where sections had not triggered yet. A flick scroll outruns it. We are mobile-first and this fails the first test.

×

Icon-circle "Core Values"

Great Services, Highest Standards, Professional Team, Creative Solutions. Four adjectives and no fact. Swap any competitor's name in and it still reads true, which is how we know it says nothing.

How to run it

  • One at a time. Say a number and it gets built, deployed and checked on a real phone before the next one starts.
  • Suggested order: 6 and 5 first, both small and both close gaps we can fill today. Then 1, which needs photos chosen. Then 2, which waits on the review outreach.
  • 3 needs one answer from you before it ships: are we comfortable showing the hardware makers' logos.
  • Nothing here touches the stack. Next.js, same repo, same deploy, same gates.
