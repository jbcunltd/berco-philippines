Re-finishing our own renders

One source, one instruction, three models. Can they change the finish without redrawing the interior?

19 Aug 2026 · 1.24 credits · balance 431

The instruction, identical to all three

Keep the drawer construction, wire baskets, plate rack, cups and pot lids exactly as they are, in the same positions. Change only the cabinet doors and drawer fronts to Charcoal Ink — a deep matte charcoal woodgrain. ARRI Alexa 35, Zeiss Supreme Prime 40mm, T2.8. Fine even grain. No lettering, numerals or maker's marks anywhere; no printed matter, packaging, labels or signage in frame.

The source

ACC_p014 — one of our seven mark-audited clean renders

The three attempts

Soul Cinema

soul_cinematic
Discarded the source entirely

Rotated the composition roughly 90°, invented a different kitchen, turned the fronts pale rather than charcoal, and dropped a white blob into the top-left corner. Nothing of the source survives.

Soul 2.0

soul_v2
Plausible — but a different kitchen

A believable showroom photograph in its own right, and it did produce drawers. But it is not our render re-finished — different drawers, different contents, different cabinet above, and the fronts came back taupe, not charcoal. The source was a hint, not a reference.

Nano Banana

nano_banana
Held everything. Changed only the fronts

Wire baskets, plate rack, the blue-triangle cups, the pot lids, the colander, the glass cabinet above, the worktop, the floor — all preserved in position. Only the doors and drawer fronts changed, to a convincing charcoal woodgrain.

What this settles

  • Soul cannot do this. Neither variant holds a reference image — its medias role is a hint, not structural conditioning. Both invented a new scene.
  • Nano Banana can, and did it almost perfectly. Its role is literally image_references, and it behaves like it. This is the tool for re-finishing.
  • So the plan works — with the other model. Our seven clean renders can each be re-finished into any of the 202 named Berco finishes, keeping the real geometry.
  • Cost: 1 credit per re-finish. Seven scenes across three schemes is 21 credits. Trivial against 431.
  • Mild colour drift is still present — the worktop cooled slightly and the cabinet interior warmed. The sampled-colour anchor still applies.
