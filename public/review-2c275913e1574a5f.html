USABLE

Pegboard, hooks, brushes, towel and hinges all held. Walnut reads convincingly, and the dark board against warm wood gives real tonal contrast. It also outpainted more room — marble splashback, fluted glass cabinet — rather than cropping.

Divided drawer

USABLE

This is the render our own audit flagged as “grey-on-white — low tonal contrast is the losing reel’s look” and dropped from Reel A. A dark finish fixes exactly that fault. Dividers, jars and containers all preserved. ⚠️ But the packaging came through — see below.

3

Plate rack + knife block
→ Honey Oak
USABLE

Plate rack, knife block, utensils, wire baskets and pot lids all intact. Clean and bright. The most catalogue-like of the six — useful as a calm beat between stronger frames.

4

Tall larder, dark room
→ Alabaster Grain
USABLE AFTER A CROP

Flipped a dark kitchen to daylight and held the larder, shelves and bottles. But there is a black diagonal artefact at top right that has to be cropped out before use.

5

Aerial dish drawer, with person
→ Deep Space Grey
BEST OF THE SIX

The best of the six. Person, pose, hands, the plates she is lifting, the dividers and every utensil preserved — and the grey fronts against the white worktop give it contrast the original lacked. Mark-checked clean at 4×.

6

Tall larder, black ground
→ Cloud White
REJECT — KEEP THE ORIGINAL

Technically it did what was asked. Creatively it destroyed the frame — a huge empty white field with the larder shoved to the right edge. The original’s power was the black ground. Keep the original; don’t re-finish this one.

The mark gate — where it slipped

PACKAGING SURVIVED — AND IT WAS MY PROMPT'S FAULT

Cardboard boxes carrying illegible pseudo-text and invented label art. Not a recognisable brand — but by our own rule, illegible is not a pass.

SAME IN THE LARDER

Bottles and jars with pseudo-labels throughout.

What this run settled

  • The method works. Four of six are usable now, one after a crop. Our seven clean renders can carry a whole reel without repeating a finish.
  • The square default helped rather than hurt. Omitting the aspect ratio made Nano Banana outpaint — we gained room context instead of losing composition. Worth keeping.
  • A dark finish rescues a render we had written off. Scene 2 was dropped from Reel A for low tonal contrast. Gilded Ebony fixes the exact fault — that is a technique, not a one-off.
  • But don't flip a dark hero to white. Scene 6 was our most dramatic frame and Cloud White emptied it. Tonal contrast was the asset; the finish swap spent it.
  • ⚠️ My prompt contradicted itself. I asked it to preserve the jars, boxes and containers and to include no printed matter in the same breath. The preserve-clause won. You cannot preserve an object class and exclude it at once — when the source contains packaging, the instruction must be to remove and replace it, not merely to not add it. Cheap to re-run.
  • Cost: 1 credit a scene. Six re-finishes for 6 credits. The whole look of a reel is now a rounding error against one 45-credit clip.
