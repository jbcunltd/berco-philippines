The mark gate

SCENE 3 — CLEAN AT 5×

Jar lids, knife handles, chopping boards and the cutlery holder all clear. No lettering.

SCENES 4 AND 6 — PSEUDO-LABELS ON THE SHELVES

Bottles, jars and boxes carrying invented, illegible labels. No recognisable brand — but by our own rule illegible is not a pass, because a video model can sharpen a smudge into something readable.

The judgement: a larder is definitionally full of packaged goods, so “remove the packaging” fights the subject. These two are safe as stills with a Ken Burns move, and should not be fed to Seedance without a tighter crop first.

Where this leaves us

  • Four scenes are ready — 1, 2, 3 and 5. Two more are usable as stills only.
  • 9:16 composes better than square. Nano Banana framed for the format rather than cropping into it, and the tall larder in particular suits the vertical frame.
  • The packaging fix worked where the subject allowed it. Telling it to remove and replace cleared the boxes from scene 2. It cannot clear a larder, because that is what a larder is.
  • A dark finish rescues a low-contrast render. Scene 2 was written off; Gilded Ebony fixed the exact fault it was dropped for.
  • Don't flip a dark hero to white. Cloud White emptied scene 6 last pass. Cedar Green on the same dark ground made it the best frame in the set.
  • Crop the floor. Scenes 2 and 5 both carry a lot of empty ground at the bottom — free to fix in ffmpeg.
