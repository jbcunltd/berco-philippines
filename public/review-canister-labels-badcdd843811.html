What I can and cannot do

The text sits in the middle of the subject, on the product itself. Cropping it out removes the product. Patching it would mean painting over the canister face, which is a bigger invention than the mark removal we did elsewhere today. So the honest options are to leave it, to pull the two photographs, or to look for a clean alternative in the supplier library.
